# English Flashcards

## Getting started
On first launch three decks are added: 300 most common words, 150 words for IELTS Academic and
100 computer graphics terms. On the **Home** page click **Start studying**.

## Study modes
- **Flip**: look at the word, recall it, press `Space` to reveal the answer and grade yourself:
  `1` Again, `2` Hard, `3` Good, `4` Easy. Each button shows when the card will come back.
- **Type**: the Uzbek translation is shown; type the English word and press `Enter`.
- **Choice**: pick the correct translation out of four (`1`–`4`).
- **Listen**: the word is spoken; type what you hear. Press `P` to hear it again.

## How scheduling works
The app uses the SM-2 algorithm. Words you know well come back at growing intervals (1, 6, 15 days ...),
forgotten words return the next day. New cards per day and the daily goal are set in **Settings**.

## Cards
On the **Cards** page search, add (`Ctrl+N`), edit (`F2`), delete (`Del`) or move cards to another deck with
a right-click. A card can have an image.

## Import and export
Import CSV, Excel (.xlsx) and Anki TSV files; a dialog lets you map the columns.
Export a deck to CSV, Excel or TSV.

## Pronunciation
The built-in Windows speech engine is used, no internet needed. If there is no English voice, add
English (United States) in Windows Settings, Time & language, Speech.
